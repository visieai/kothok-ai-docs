# Branding & "Powered by"

You can make the widget look like part of your own brand. Some brand controls are
included on paid plans. This page explains which is which.

## What you can change on every plan

These work on all plans, including Free:

* **Colour** — set your main brand colour.
* **Position** — choose which side the chat button sits on.
* **Greeting** — set the first message.
* **Placeholder** — set the grey text in the message box.
* **Quick links** — add shortcut links to the home screen.

## What comes with paid plans

These need a paid plan:

* **Custom title and logo.** On Free and Starter plans, the chat header shows the
  default Kothok AI title and logo. On Growth and higher, you can set your own. See
  [Plans & billing](../billing/plans.md).
* **Remove "Powered by Kothok".** The chat box shows a small "Powered by Kothok"
  line. Growth and higher plans can hide it.

!!! note
    The "Powered by" line is controlled by your plan, not by the page code. You cannot
    remove it by editing the script tag on a plan that does not include it.

## How to set your title and logo

1. Open the **Installation** or **Settings** page in the dashboard.
2. Set your **title**.
3. Upload your **logo**. Allowed types are PNG, JPEG, GIF, WEBP, and SVG, up to 2 MB.
4. Save.

**Result:** your title and logo appear in the chat header, including on sites already
running the widget.

If your plan does not include custom branding, these controls prompt you to upgrade.
See [Plans & billing](../billing/plans.md).
