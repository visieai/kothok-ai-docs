# Train your assistant

Before the assistant can help anyone, it needs to know about your business. You teach
it by giving it your own content — your website, your files, your questions and
answers. The assistant reads all of it and answers your visitors from what it
learned. It does not make things up, and it does not use anyone else's information.

This is called your **knowledge base**. You manage it on the **Knowledge Base** page
in the dashboard.

## The four ways to add knowledge

| Source | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Website** | Everything already on your site | The assistant reads your pages for you |
| **Files** | Brochures, guides, price lists, policies | PDF, Word, or text files |
| **Custom answers** | Exact answers to common questions | You write the question and the answer |
| **Fix a missed question** | Gaps you find over time | Answer a question the assistant could not |

You can use any mix of these. Most businesses start with their website, then add a
few files and custom answers.

Step-by-step for each is in [Add knowledge](add-knowledge.md).

## How the assistant uses it

When a visitor asks something, the assistant looks through your knowledge, finds the
part that fits, and writes an answer in its own words based on it. If it cannot find
anything, it says so rather than guessing.

## Keep it fresh

Your business changes — prices, hours, policies. When it does, update your knowledge
so the assistant stays right. See [Keep it updated](keep-updated.md).

## A note on plans

Adding your website and files works on every plan. Some tools — writing your own
**custom answers**, and the **missed-question review** — are part of the paid plans.
