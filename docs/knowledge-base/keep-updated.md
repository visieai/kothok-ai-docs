# Keep it updated

The assistant only knows what you have given it. When your business changes, update
your knowledge so the answers stay correct.

## When to update

- You changed your prices, hours, or policies.
- You added a new service or product line.
- You noticed the assistant gave an old or wrong answer.

## How to update

- **Website changed?** Re-add or refresh your website so the assistant reads the new
  pages.
- **New document?** Upload the new file, and delete the old one so the assistant does
  not use both.
- **Want an exact answer?** Add or edit a [custom answer](add-knowledge.md).
- **Assistant missed something?** Use the missed-questions review to fill the gap.

## Test after every change

After you change your knowledge, test it:

1. Open your site (or the **Try your AI** page in the dashboard).
2. Ask the question you just changed.
3. Check the answer is now correct.

## Test before customers do — "Try your AI"

The dashboard has a **Try your AI** page. It is a private chat where you can ask the
assistant anything and see how it answers, without touching your live site. Use it
whenever you add knowledge or before you go live.

## One thing to know about deleting custom answers

When you delete a custom answer, it is removed from your list right away. In some
cases the assistant may still recall it briefly until the knowledge fully refreshes.
If an old custom answer keeps showing up, contact [Support](../support.md).
