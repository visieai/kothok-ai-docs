# Add knowledge

All of this is done on the **Knowledge Base** page in the dashboard. Here is each
source, step by step.

## Add your website

The fastest way to start. The assistant reads your website for you.

1. On the Knowledge Base page, choose **Add website** and enter your web address.
2. The assistant visits your site and reads your pages, following links a couple of
   levels deep to cover the important pages.
3. This runs in the background. You can watch the progress and come back when it is
   done.

Good to know:

- You connect **one website** per account.
- A website already used by another Kothok AI account cannot be added again.
- Nothing is changed on your site. The assistant only reads it.

## Upload files

Add brochures, guides, policies, or price lists.

- **Allowed types:** PDF, Word (`.docx`), and plain text (`.txt`).
- **Size:** up to 5 MB per file. For a bigger file, split it or save a smaller
  version.
- Scanned PDFs (pictures of text) are read automatically and turned into text the
  assistant can use.

Upload a file, and once it is processed the assistant can answer from it.

## Write custom answers

Custom answers are question-and-answer pairs you write yourself. Use them when you
want the assistant to give an exact answer to a common question — your return policy,
your delivery time, your opening hours.

1. On the Knowledge Base page, open **Custom answers**.
2. Add the question a customer would ask, and the exact answer you want given.
3. Save. The assistant now uses your wording for that question.

You can edit or delete custom answers at any time.

!!! note
    Custom answers are part of the paid plans. See [Plans & billing](../billing/plans.md).

## Fix a question the assistant missed

Over time, visitors will ask things your knowledge does not cover. The dashboard
collects these **missed questions** so you can fill the gaps.

1. Open the missed-questions (knowledge-gap) review on the Knowledge Base page. It
   shows recent questions the assistant could not answer.
2. Pick one and write the answer.
3. Save. The assistant can now answer it next time.

This is the best way to make the assistant better week by week. It, too, is part of
the paid plans.

## See and manage your sources

The Knowledge Base page lists every source you have added — websites and files
alike. For each one you can:

- **Preview** what the assistant learned from it.
- **Delete** it if it is out of date.

After you delete or change a source, test the assistant to make sure the old answer
is gone. See [Keep it updated](keep-updated.md).
