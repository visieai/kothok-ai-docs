# Where it works

The assistant answers on your website and on chat apps. The answers are the same.
The way products are shown is different.

## Your website

On your website the shopper sees product cards — photo, price, stock, and a link.
The cards appear inside the chat widget you add to your site. See
[Install the widget](../developer/widget.md).

## WhatsApp and Messenger

On WhatsApp and Messenger there are no cards. The assistant writes the same products
out as text, with the name, price, and link on separate lines.

The shopper gets the same answer either way. Only the look changes.

## What is the same everywhere

- It finds the same products.
- It follows the same budget and size filters.
- It never makes up a product.
- It answers review questions where reviews are available.

## Setting up chat apps

Connecting WhatsApp or Messenger is a separate setup step. Contact us and we will
walk you through it. See [Support](../support.md).
