# What's coming

These are known gaps we plan to close. They are listed roughly in the order we plan
to build them. Dates are not promised here — ask us for the latest timing.

## Automatic re-sync

Today you press **Sync** after a price or stock change. We are adding a scheduled
refresh so the assistant updates on its own. This is the top priority, because it
removes the one manual step every store has to remember.

## Direct Shopify connection

Today a Shopify store is read by crawling its pages, which guesses at sizes and
stock. A direct connection will read exact variants, stock, and sale prices straight
from Shopify.

## Order tracking

"Where is my order?" is the most common shopping question, and the assistant cannot
answer it yet. This is planned.

## Sale prices shown as "was / now"

Today a sale price becomes part of a price range. We plan to show a real discount,
with the old and new price, and mark the product as on sale.

## Answer ratings

A thumbs up / down on each answer, so we can find and fix weak answers.
