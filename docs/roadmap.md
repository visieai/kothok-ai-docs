# What's coming

These are features we are working on or planning. Dates are not promised here — ask
us for the latest timing. See [Support](support.md).

## Across the product

* **Telegram channel.** Answer messages on Telegram, alongside WhatsApp and
  Messenger.
* **More automation and workflows.** Let the assistant take actions, not only answer.
* **Deeper analytics.** More detail on how your assistant is performing.

## For online stores

* **Automatic catalogue refresh.** Today you press **Sync** after a price or stock
  change. We are adding a scheduled refresh so it updates on its own. See
  [Keeping your catalogue current](selling/keeping-current.md).
* **Direct Shopify connection.** Read exact variants, stock, and sale prices straight
  from Shopify.
* **Order tracking.** Answer "where is my order?" in chat.
* **Sale prices shown as "was / now".** Show a real discount instead of a price
  range. See [What it will not do](selling/limitations.md).

## Tell us what matters

If one of these is important to your launch, let us know. Your feedback helps us
decide what to build next. See [Support](support.md).
