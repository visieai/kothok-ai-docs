# Leads

When a visitor shares their contact details in a chat, the assistant saves them as a
**lead** so you can follow up. You find leads on the **Leads** page in the dashboard.

## What is captured

The assistant picks up details a visitor gives during a chat, such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Company size
* What they are looking for

It also adds a **lead score** and a **buying-intent score** to help you spot the most
promising leads, and a suggested **next best action**.

## Find your leads

1. Open the **Leads** page in the dashboard.
2. Browse the list, or open one lead to see the full details.
3. From a lead, open the linked conversation to read exactly what the visitor asked.
   See [Conversations & the inbox](inbox.md).

## Tickets

Alongside leads, the assistant can create **tickets** from conversations — for
example, a support request that needs follow-up. Find these on the **Tickets** page,
where you can add tags and notes and assign each ticket to a team member.
