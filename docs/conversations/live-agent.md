# Reply as a human

The assistant handles most chats on its own. But when a person needs to take over —
a tricky question, a complaint, a sale you want to close yourself — you can reply in
the same conversation. This works on your **website**, **WhatsApp**, and
**Messenger**.

## How to take over

1. Open the conversation in the [inbox](inbox.md).
2. Type your reply and send it.
3. Your message reaches the visitor, and the assistant goes quiet in that chat.

From that point, the visitor is talking to you. Their messages keep showing up in the
conversation so you can answer them.

## What the visitor sees

- On the **website**, your reply appears in the chat right away if the visitor still
  has the page open. If they closed the tab, they see it the next time they open the
  chat during that visit.
- On **WhatsApp** and **Messenger**, your reply arrives as a normal message.

## Handing back to the assistant

When you are done, click **Resume bot** (hand back to the assistant) on the
conversation. The assistant takes over again and answers new messages.

## Good to know

- **The assistant stays quiet while you are handling a chat.** It will not answer
  over you.
- **On the website, there is a short grace period.** After your last message, the
  chat is held open for a while so a slow reply from the visitor does not end the
  conversation in the middle of your help.
- **If the visitor never comes back**, a website reply you sent while they were away
  waits in the chat history for their next visit. There is no email fallback for
  website chats today, so for anything urgent, use a channel where you have their
  contact details.
