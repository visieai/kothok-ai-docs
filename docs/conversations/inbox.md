# Conversations & the inbox

Every chat your assistant has is saved in the dashboard. The **inbox** is where you
read those chats, see who is talking to you, and step in when you want to.

## Reading your chats

Open the **Conversations** (inbox) page. You see a list of chats, newest activity
first. Click any one to read the full back-and-forth between the visitor and the
assistant.

## Who is online

For chats on your **website**, the inbox shows whether the visitor is online right
now. This helps you jump in while they are still on the page. Chats on WhatsApp and
Messenger do not show an online status, because those apps do not share it.

## Stepping in as a human

Sometimes a person should answer instead of the assistant. You can reply yourself,
right inside the same conversation. See [Reply as a human](live-agent.md).

## Leads and contact details

When a visitor shares details — like a name, email, or phone number — the assistant
captures them so you have a way to follow up. You can find these on the conversation
and in your leads. See [Leads](leads.md).

## What is saved

- The full message history of each chat.
- Any contact details the visitor shared.
- Any meeting the assistant booked in that chat.
- Whether a human took over.
