# What shoppers can ask

The assistant answers shoppers who are looking for something to buy. They can type
the way they talk. They do not need to know the right words or the exact product
name.

## Finding products

A shopper can ask in plain language:

> "waterproof running shoes under 3000 taka, size 42"

The assistant reads your product list and replies with a short line, then shows the
matching products as cards. Each card has:

- the product photo,
- the price,
- whether it is in stock,
- a link straight to the product page.

It understands budgets and details. "Under 3000 taka" means everything it shows is
really under 3000. "Size 42" means it only shows products in that size, when your
list carries size.

## Asking how good a product is

The assistant answers questions like "are these comfortable?" using a short summary
built from that product's real customer reviews.

The answer comes from your reviews, not from an opinion the assistant made up. If
your reviews say the sizing runs small, that is what it tells the shopper.

!!! note
    Review answers work only for [Website and Feed](connect-your-store.md)
    stores, because reviews are read off your product pages.

## When it cannot find a match

If nothing matches, the assistant says so. It does not guess, and it does not offer
a product you do not sell. See [What it will not do](limitations.md).
