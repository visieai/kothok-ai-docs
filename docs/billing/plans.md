# Plans

Kothok AI has five plans. Every new account starts on **Free**. You can upgrade or
change plan at any time from the **Billing** page in the dashboard.

Only the account **owner** and **admins** can see and change billing. See
[Team members & roles](../team/members-roles.md).

## Plans at a glance

Prices are per month. Yearly billing costs about ten percent less.

| Plan | Price (BDT / USD) | Conversations / month | Team seats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 0 / 0 | 50 | 1 |
| **Starter** | 1,000 / 19 | 300 | 1 |
| **Growth** | 2,800 / 49 | 800 | 3 |
| **Business** | 5,500 / 99 | 1,800 | 8 |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Prices may change. The **Billing** page always shows the current amounts.

## What each plan adds

Higher plans unlock more tools. Here are the main differences.

**Free**

* Answer questions from your website and files.
* One team member (you).
* Default Kothok AI branding on the widget.

**Starter** adds:

* More knowledge and manual updates.
* Custom answers and missed-question review. See [Train your assistant](../knowledge-base/overview.md).
* Lead capture. See [Leads](../conversations/leads.md).
* Email support.

**Growth** adds:

* Automatic knowledge updates.
* Custom widget title and logo, and the option to remove "Powered by Kothok". See
  [Branding & "Powered by"](../widget/branding.md).
* Live human reply on the website widget. See [Reply as a human](../conversations/live-agent.md).
* Advanced analytics and priority support.
* Three team seats.

**Business** adds:

* Custom assistant behaviour.
* API access and automation.
* Eight team seats.

**Enterprise** adds:

* Unlimited conversations and team seats.
* A dedicated account manager, a service-level agreement, and custom integrations.

## Conversations and limits

Each plan includes a number of conversations per month. When you need more, buy an
add-on pack instead of upgrading. See [Usage & limits](usage-and-limits.md) and
[Add-ons](add-ons.md).

## Change your plan

1. Open the **Billing** page in the dashboard.
2. Choose a plan and a billing cycle (monthly or yearly).
3. Enter a discount code if you have one. See [Discounts](discounts.md).
4. Pay. See [How to pay](payments.md).

**Result:** your new plan and its limits apply right away.
