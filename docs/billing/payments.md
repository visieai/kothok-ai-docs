# How to pay

Kothok AI accepts payment in two currencies. The currency decides which payment
methods you see at checkout.

## Currencies and methods

| Currency | Payment methods | Who can use it |
|---|---|---|
| **BDT** (৳) | bKash, Nagad, cards, and bank transfer | Businesses in Bangladesh |
| **USD** ($) | International cards | Everyone else |

Businesses in Bangladesh can choose either currency. Everyone else pays in USD.

## Before you pay

* Add a billing email to your account. Your receipt goes there.
* Choose monthly or yearly billing. Yearly costs about ten percent less.

## Pay in BDT

1. On the **Billing** page, choose your plan or add-on and select **BDT**.
2. Select **Pay**. You are taken to the secure checkout page.
3. Choose bKash, Nagad, a card, or bank transfer, and complete the payment.
4. You return to the dashboard once the payment is confirmed.

**Result:** your plan or add-on is active, and a receipt is available to download.

## Pay in USD

1. On the **Billing** page, choose your plan or add-on and select **USD**.
2. Select **Pay**. You are taken to the secure checkout page.
3. Enter your card details and complete the payment.
4. You return to the dashboard once the payment is confirmed.

**Result:** your plan or add-on is active, and an invoice is available to download.

## Receipts and invoices

* Find your past payments on the **Billing** page.
* For BDT payments, you can download a receipt.
* For USD payments, you can download an invoice.

## Manage a USD subscription

If you pay in USD, you can open a billing portal from the **Billing** page to update
your card, switch plans, or view invoices.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled by our team. If you need one, contact [Support](../support.md).
There is no automatic refund button today.
