# Usage & limits

Your plan includes a number of **conversations** each month. This page explains what
counts as a conversation and how to see your usage.

## What counts as a conversation

* A conversation is counted the first time a visitor sends a message in a chat.
* Later messages in the same chat do not count again.
* Conversations count the same on every channel — website, WhatsApp, and Messenger.
* If you use an **AI-suggested reply** to help you answer, that suggestion counts as
  one conversation.

## See your usage

Open the **Billing** page in the dashboard. It shows:

* How many conversations you have used this month.
* How many you have left.
* Any extra conversations from add-on packs.

## Warnings and limits

* You get a warning when you reach about 80 percent of your monthly limit.
* You get another warning at 100 percent.
* When you run out, the assistant pauses new conversations until you add more or your
  month resets.

## When you need more

You have two choices:

1. **Buy an add-on pack** for a quick top-up. See [Add-ons](add-ons.md).
2. **Upgrade your plan** for a higher monthly limit. See [Plans](plans.md).

Add-on conversations stay on your account until you use them. They do not disappear
when the month resets.

## WhatsApp conversations

Some plans include a separate cap for WhatsApp conversations. You can top these up
with a WhatsApp add-on pack. See [Add-ons](add-ons.md).
