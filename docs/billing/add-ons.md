# Add-ons

Add-ons are one-time packs that top up your conversations without changing your plan.
Buy one when you are close to your monthly limit but do not want to upgrade.

## Available packs

| Pack | What you get | Price (BDT / USD) |
|---|---|---|
| **Extra conversations (100)** | 100 more conversations | 400 / 5 |
| **Extra conversations (300)** | 300 more conversations | 1,200 / 15 |
| **Extra WhatsApp (100)** | 100 more WhatsApp conversations | 500 / — |

The WhatsApp pack is available for accounts billed in BDT.

## How add-on conversations work

* They are added to your account as soon as your payment is confirmed.
* They **do not expire** at the end of the month. They stay until you use them.
* Your plan's monthly conversations are used first; add-on conversations are used
  after those run out.

## Buy an add-on

1. Open the **Billing** page in the dashboard.
2. Find **Add-ons** and choose a pack.
3. Enter a discount code if you have one. See [Discounts](discounts.md).
4. Pay. See [How to pay](payments.md).

**Result:** the extra conversations appear in your usage right away.

## Not sure whether to buy an add-on or upgrade?

* **Add-on** — a one-time top-up for a busy month.
* **Upgrade** — a higher monthly limit every month, plus more features. See [Plans](plans.md).
