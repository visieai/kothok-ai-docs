# Team members & roles

You can invite people to help run your assistant. This page explains the roles and
how to add or remove team members.

## Roles

There are three roles:

* **Owner** — the person who created the account. Full control, including billing.
  There is one owner.
* **Admin** — can manage the team and settings, and see billing.
* **Member** — can do everyday work like reading chats and replying, but cannot
  manage the team or billing.

Only the **owner** and **admins** can invite people or change roles.

## How many people can you add

The number of team members depends on your plan.

| Plan | Team seats |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 (owner only) |
| Starter | 1 (owner only) |
| Growth | 3 |
| Business | 8 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

On Free and Starter, only the owner has access. To invite teammates, upgrade to
Growth or higher. See [Plans](../billing/plans.md).

## Invite someone

1. Open the **Team** page in the dashboard.
2. Select **Invite**, and enter the person's email address and role.
3. Send the invite. They receive an email with a link.

The invite link is valid for about seven days. If it expires, you can resend it from
the **Team** page.

**Result:** once they accept and set a password, they join your account.

## One account per person

An email address can belong to only one Kothok AI account. If someone is already part
of another account, they cannot be added to yours with the same email.

## Change a role or remove someone

* **Change a role** — on the **Team** page, change a member between admin and member.
* **Remove someone** — remove a member from the **Team** page. You cannot remove
  yourself or the owner.

When you remove a member, any tickets assigned to them are unassigned so nothing is
lost.
