# How onboarding works

Onboarding is the path from "signed up" to "the assistant answers product questions
correctly." It has four steps. You can do most of it yourself. We do the last check
with you.

## The four steps

1. **You give us your store's web address.** Takes a minute.
2. **We show you one of your products.** You confirm the name and price are right.
3. **We bring in the rest.** This runs in the background. A few hundred products
   take a few minutes. Customer reviews come in a little after, so do not worry if
   review answers are not there straight away.
4. **We check it together before any shopper sees it.** This takes about ten
   minutes. See the [go-live checklist](go-live-checklist.md).

## What we need from you

Just your store's web address to begin.

We look at your site, find your products, and show you one of them. You check that
one product looks right — the correct name, the correct price. If it does, we bring
in the rest.

If your site is one we cannot read automatically, we ask for a product file instead:
export your products as a CSV from your shop admin and send it. There are two more
ways in for larger stores or stores with a developer. All of them are covered in
[Connect your store](connect-your-store.md).

## One product list per store

We work from one product list. If you connect your website now and switch to a file
later, the file replaces what was there. We cannot join two lists together.

## What happens next

Once the checklist passes, a developer (or you, if it is a simple site) adds one
line of code to your website. See [Install the widget](../developer/widget.md).

After you go live, remember the one manual step: press **Sync** whenever your
prices or stock change. See [Keeping your catalogue current](keeping-current.md).
