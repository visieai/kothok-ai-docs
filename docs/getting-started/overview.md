# Getting started

This section is for most businesses — you want an assistant that answers your
visitors' questions, and maybe books meetings for you.

!!! tip "Run an online store?"
    If your main goal is to help shoppers find and buy products, follow the separate
    [Selling products](../selling/overview.md) guide instead. It is written for online
    stores and covers everything a shop needs, start to finish. You can still use the
    steps below as well.

## What you will do

Setting up takes four steps. None of them need code, except pasting one line onto
your website (a developer can do that part in a minute).

1. **Create your account** and sign in to the dashboard.
2. **Train your assistant** — give it your website, files, or FAQs so it can answer
   questions about your business. See [Train your assistant](../knowledge-base/overview.md).
3. **Add the chat box to your website** — one line of code. See
   [Install the widget](install-widget.md).
4. **Turn on the extra tools you want** — book meetings, connect WhatsApp or
   Messenger, and so on.

Each step is covered on its own page. Do them in order the first time.

## Step 1 — Create your account

Sign up and sign in. You land on the **dashboard** — the control panel where you
manage everything. Take a moment to look around; the [Dashboard tour](dashboard-tour.md)
explains each part.

## Step 2 — Train your assistant

Before the assistant can help anyone, it needs to know about your business.

You give it knowledge from sources you already have — your website address, a few
files, or a list of questions and answers. The assistant reads them and answers your
visitors from what it learned. It does not make things up.

Full steps are in [Train your assistant](../knowledge-base/overview.md).

## Step 3 — Add the chat box to your website

The chat box is a small script you paste into your website, just before the closing
`</body>` tag. Once it is there, the chat button appears in the corner of your site.

See [Install the widget](install-widget.md). You can change its colour, title, logo,
and more — see [Customize the widget](../widget/install.md).

## Step 4 — Turn on the extra tools

With the basics working, add what you need:

- **Book meetings.** Connect your Google Calendar and let the assistant offer your
  free times. See [Booking meetings](../appointments/overview.md).
- **Reach more channels.** Connect WhatsApp and Messenger so people can chat with you
  there too. See [Channels](../channels/overview.md).
- **Reply as a human.** Read your chats and step in when needed. See
  [Conversations & the inbox](../conversations/inbox.md).

## Before you go live

Test the assistant yourself first:

1. Open your site and ask it three real questions your customers ask.
2. Check the answers are correct and match your business.
3. If an answer is wrong or missing, add or fix the knowledge and try again. See
   [Keep it updated](../knowledge-base/keep-updated.md).

When the answers look right, you are ready for real visitors.
