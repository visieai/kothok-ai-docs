# Go-live checklist

Do this before any real shopper uses the assistant. It takes about ten minutes and
it is worth the time. We do it with you.

Do not go live until all five checks pass.

## 1. Is the product count right?

Tell us roughly how many products you sell. We compare it with the number we
brought in.

If our number is half or double yours, something was read wrong. We fix it before
going on. A count that is a little off usually means a few products were skipped;
a count that is far off means the list was read wrong.

## 2. Are the prices right?

Pick any three products yourself and compare them with your live site.

Check the prices yourself rather than taking our word for it. A wrong price is the
one mistake that costs real money. Also check the currency is the one you expect.

## 3. Do the links work?

Click three product cards. Each one should open the correct product page on your
site.

## 4. Does a budget work?

Ask the assistant for something under a price, like "shoes under 2000 taka."

Everything it shows must actually be under that price. This is the check most worth
doing, because it is the one that is easy to get wrong.

## 5. Does it say no?

Ask for something you definitely do not sell.

It should say it has nothing like that. It must not offer something else instead.
An assistant that makes up a product is worse than no assistant, so this check
matters most of all.

## When all five pass

You are ready to go live. Next:

- [Install the widget](../developer/widget.md) on your site.
- Set your plan for [keeping the catalogue current](keeping-current.md).
