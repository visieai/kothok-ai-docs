# First-time setup

When you sign up, a short setup wizard walks you through creating your assistant. It
takes a few minutes. This page explains each step so you know what to expect.

## Prerequisites

- A sign-up account.
- One of the following to train your assistant:
    - your website address, **or**
    - a document about your business (PDF, Word, or text).

## The steps

1. **Welcome.** A short introduction. Select **Get started**.
2. **Details.** Enter your **company name** and a **name for your assistant** (the
   name shoppers and visitors see). You do not pick an industry — the assistant works
   it out from your content.
3. **Widget basics.** If you are training from a website, set a **greeting** and the
   **position** of the chat box. If you are uploading a document instead, this step is
   skipped.
4. **Add your knowledge.** Choose one:
    - **Website** — enter your web address. The assistant reads your pages.
    - **Document** — upload a file about your business.
5. **Getting ready.** A progress screen shows the assistant learning from your
   content ("Validating", "Reading", "Getting ready", "Finalizing"). Wait for it to
   finish.
6. **Go live.** The wizard shows your widget snippet so you can add the assistant to
   your site. See [Install the widget](install-widget.md).

**Result:** your assistant is trained and ready to answer questions.

## After the wizard

The wizard covers the basics. To get the most from your assistant, continue with:

- [Train your assistant](../knowledge-base/overview.md) — add more knowledge and fix
  gaps.
- [Booking meetings](../appointments/overview.md) — let it book meetings for you.
- [Channels](../channels/overview.md) — add WhatsApp and Messenger.

## If you were invited by a teammate

If someone added you to an existing account, you skip the wizard. You join their
workspace and land straight in the dashboard. See [Team members & roles](../team/members-roles.md).
