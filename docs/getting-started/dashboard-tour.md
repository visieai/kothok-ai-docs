# Dashboard tour

The dashboard is where you manage your assistant. This page explains each section so
you know where to find things. You do not need to use them all — start with the ones
you need.

## Everyday work

- **Overview** — Your home screen. Shows key numbers and highlights questions the
  assistant could not answer.
- **Conversations** — Every chat, across every channel, in one inbox. Read chats,
  reply as a human, and mark them resolved. See [Conversations & the inbox](../conversations/inbox.md).
- **Tickets** — Support tickets the assistant creates from conversations, with tags,
  notes, and an assignee.
- **Leads** — Contact details the assistant captured from visitors. See
  [Leads](../conversations/leads.md).
- **Meetings** — Meetings the assistant booked. See [Manage your bookings](../appointments/manage-bookings.md).

## Understand your performance

- **Analytics** — Charts about your chats and how the assistant is doing. Deeper
  reports come with higher plans.

## Train and test

- **Knowledge Base** — Add and manage what the assistant knows. See
  [Train your assistant](../knowledge-base/overview.md).
- **Try your AI** — A private chat to test the assistant before customers use it.

## Set up

- **Installation** — Get the widget snippet and check your setup status. See
  [Install the widget](install-widget.md).
- **Integrations** — Connect channels (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram) and Google
  Calendar. See [Channels](../channels/overview.md) and
  [Booking meetings](../appointments/overview.md).
- **Team** — Invite people and set their roles. See [Team members & roles](../team/members-roles.md).
- **Settings** — Your account and assistant settings.

## Billing

- **Billing** — Your plan, usage, add-ons, and invoices. Only the account owner and
  admins can see this. See [Plans & billing](../billing/plans.md).

## What you will not see

Some tools are for Kothok AI staff only and never appear in your dashboard, such as
the platform-wide admin area. If a guide mentions something you cannot find, check
your [plan](../billing/plans.md) and your role — some features are limited by both.
