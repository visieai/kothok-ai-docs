# Booking meetings

The assistant can book meetings for you. When a visitor wants to talk, it offers
times when you are free, takes their choice, and puts the meeting on your calendar.
Both you and the visitor get an invitation by email.

This works with **Google Calendar**. You connect your own calendar once, and every
meeting the assistant books goes onto it.

## How it works for the visitor

1. The visitor asks to book a call, or the assistant offers one.
2. The assistant shows a few available times, taken from your real calendar so it
   never offers a slot when you are busy.
3. The visitor picks a time — by tapping it on your website, or by replying with a
   number on WhatsApp or Messenger.
4. The assistant books it and the invitation goes out. If a Google Meet link is set
   up, it is included.

## What you need

- A **Google Calendar** account.
- A few minutes to connect it and set your working hours.

Start with [Connect your calendar](connect-calendar.md).

## Where booking works

Booking works on your **website widget**, **WhatsApp**, and **Messenger**.

On the website, times appear as buttons the visitor can tap. On WhatsApp and
Messenger, the assistant lists the times and the visitor replies with a number, like
"2".

## If your calendar is not connected

If you have not connected a calendar, the assistant can still take the visitor's
email so you can follow up. It will not offer specific times until a calendar is
connected.

## Managing your meetings

You can see upcoming and past meetings in the dashboard, along with which
conversation each one came from. See [Manage your bookings](manage-bookings.md).
