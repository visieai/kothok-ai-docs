# Connect your calendar

You connect Google Calendar once, from the dashboard. After that, the assistant can
read your free times and add meetings for you.

## Steps

1. In the dashboard, open the **Integrations** page.
2. Find **Google Calendar** and click **Connect**.
3. A Google sign-in window opens. Sign in with the Google account whose calendar you
   want to use.
4. Google asks you to allow Kothok AI to see when you are busy and to create events.
   Click **Allow**.
5. You return to the dashboard, now showing your account as connected.

!!! note "The \"Google hasn't verified this app\" screen"
    While our app finishes Google's review, you may see a screen saying Google has
    not verified the app. This is normal during setup. You can continue safely — the
    assistant only ever reads your free/busy times and creates the meetings you book.

## What Kothok AI can and cannot see

We ask Google for as little as possible:

- **Can see:** when you are busy or free, and the list of your calendars.
- **Can do:** create the meeting and invite the guest.
- **Cannot see:** the titles, guests, or details of your other events.

## Set your preferences

Once connected, set these on the same page:

- **Which calendar** to use, if you have more than one.
- **Your time zone**, so times are offered correctly.
- **Your working hours**, so meetings are only offered when you want them.
- **Google Meet** — turn this on to add a video link to every meeting.

## Keeping the connection healthy

Sometimes Google ends the connection — for example if you change your Google
password, remove access, or the calendar goes unused for a long time. When that
happens, the Integrations page shows a **Reconnect** message. Click it and sign in
again to fix it.

## Disconnecting

To stop the assistant using your calendar, click **Disconnect** on the Integrations
page. This removes our access at Google. The assistant then falls back to taking
emails instead of offering times.
