# Manage your bookings

The dashboard keeps a record of every meeting the assistant books.

## The meetings list

Open the **Meetings** page in the dashboard. You can see:

- **Upcoming** meetings, soonest first — your to-do list.
- **Past** meetings, newest first — your log.

You can filter the list by status, channel (website, WhatsApp, Messenger), the
visitor's email, or a single conversation.

## Meeting stats

The page also shows a short summary: how many meetings were booked in the last while,
how many are still upcoming, and how many **need attention**.

"Need attention" means a meeting was confirmed to the visitor in chat, but something
went wrong sending the notification. Those are worth checking by hand so nobody is
left without an invite.

## From a meeting back to the chat

Every meeting is linked to the conversation it came from. Open the conversation to
see exactly what the visitor asked and how the booking was made. See
[Conversations & the inbox](../conversations/inbox.md).

## Two things to watch

- **Notification failed.** The visitor was told the meeting was set, but the invite
  did not go out. Follow up with them directly.
- **Calendar needs reconnecting.** The booking failed because the calendar
  connection ended. Reconnect it on the [Integrations page](connect-calendar.md) so
  future bookings work.

## Cancelling and changes

Cancelling or moving a meeting is done in your Google Calendar for now. The
assistant does not cancel or reschedule meetings yet.
