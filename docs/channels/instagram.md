# Instagram

Connect your Instagram business account and the assistant answers people who send
you direct messages, using the same knowledge it uses everywhere else.

## Connect your account

1. In the dashboard, open **Integrations** and select **Connect Instagram**.
2. A sign-in window opens. Sign in with the Instagram business account you want to
   connect.
3. Allow Kothok AI to receive and reply to your direct messages.
4. When it finishes, your account is connected and the assistant starts replying to
   direct messages.

## Test it

From another account, send a direct message to your Instagram business account. The
assistant should reply using your [knowledge base](../knowledge-base/overview.md).

## What works today

* The assistant answers **text** direct messages automatically.
* A person on your team can [take over the chat](../conversations/live-agent.md).
* Conversations on Instagram count toward your plan, like every other channel.

## What is different

* **Products are shown as text**, not as cards.
* **Meeting booking is turned off by default on Instagram.** If you want the
  assistant to book meetings from Instagram, contact [Support](../support.md).

## Trouble connecting?

If the assistant does not reply after connecting, contact [Support](../support.md).
