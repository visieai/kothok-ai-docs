# WhatsApp

Connect your business WhatsApp number and the assistant answers people who message
you there, using the same knowledge it uses on your website.

## Connect your number

Connecting is done from the dashboard. You do not copy any tokens or codes by hand.

1. In the dashboard, open **Integrations** (or **Channels**) and click
   **Connect WhatsApp**.
2. A Facebook window opens. Sign in with the Facebook account that manages your
   business.
3. Pick or create your **WhatsApp Business Account**, then choose or add the phone
   number you want to use, and verify it. This all happens inside Facebook's own
   screens.
4. When the window finishes, your number is connected and the assistant starts
   answering messages to it.

## Test it

Send a WhatsApp message to your connected business number from another phone. The
assistant should reply within a few seconds, using your
[knowledge base](../knowledge-base/overview.md).

## What works today

- The assistant answers **text** messages automatically.
- It can **book meetings** if you have connected a calendar. Times are listed as a
  numbered list, and the visitor replies with a number.
- Replies happen inside WhatsApp's **24-hour service window** — that is, the
  assistant can reply for 24 hours after a customer's message. This follows
  WhatsApp's own rules.
- Conversations on WhatsApp count toward your plan, like every other channel.

## What is not supported yet

- **Sending the first message.** The assistant replies to people who message you; it
  cannot start a conversation or send marketing/template messages.
- **Images, voice notes, stickers, location, and buttons.** If someone sends an
  image or sticker, the assistant replies that it can only read text right now.
- **Product cards.** On WhatsApp, products are written out as text with a link, not
  shown as cards.

## Trouble connecting?

If there is no reply after connecting, contact us — a couple of settings on the
Facebook side may still need to be switched on for your account. See
[Support](../support.md).
