# Messenger

Connect your Facebook page and the assistant answers people who message your page on
Facebook Messenger, using the same knowledge it uses everywhere else.

## Connect your page

1. In the dashboard, open **Integrations** (or **Channels**) and click
   **Connect Messenger** (Facebook page).
2. A Facebook window opens. Sign in and choose the **Facebook page** you want to
   connect.
3. Allow Kothok AI to receive and reply to messages for that page.
4. When it finishes, your page is connected and the assistant starts replying to
   messages sent to it.

## Test it

From another Facebook account, send a message to your page. The assistant should
reply using your [knowledge base](../knowledge-base/overview.md).

## What works today

- The assistant answers **text** messages automatically.
- It can **book meetings** if you have connected a calendar. Times are listed as a
  numbered list, and the visitor replies with a number.
- A person on your team can [take over the chat](../conversations/live-agent.md).
- Conversations on Messenger count toward your plan, like every other channel.

## What is different from the website

- **Products are shown as text**, with a name, price, and link — not as cards.
- **Meeting times are a numbered list** the visitor replies to, not tappable
  buttons.

## Trouble connecting?

If the assistant does not reply after connecting, contact [Support](../support.md).
