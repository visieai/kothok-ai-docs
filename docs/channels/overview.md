# Channels

A "channel" is a place where people can chat with your assistant. Kothok AI works on
several channels today. You can use one, or all of them. The assistant gives the same
answers on each — only the look changes.

| Channel | Where it appears | Setup |
|---|---|---|
| **Website** | A chat box on your site | Paste one line of code |
| **WhatsApp** | Your business WhatsApp number | Connect from the dashboard |
| **Messenger** | Your Facebook page | Connect from the dashboard |
| **Instagram** | Direct messages to your account | Connect from the dashboard |

Telegram is planned but not available yet. See the [roadmap](../roadmap.md).

## Which to use

- **Website** is the place to start. It reaches everyone who visits your site, and
  it is the only channel that shows rich cards for products and tappable time slots
  for meetings.
- **WhatsApp** and **Messenger** meet customers where they already chat. On these,
  products and meeting times are written out as text instead of cards.

## One thing that is the same everywhere

Everything the assistant knows and does — your knowledge, your products, your
calendar — works on every channel. You set those up once, and they apply to all
channels at the same time. Every conversation, on any channel, counts toward your
plan. See [Plans & billing](../billing/plans.md).
